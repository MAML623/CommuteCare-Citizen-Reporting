Reporting Guidelines

Follow these simple guidelines to create clear, accurate, and helpful reports about problems in public transportation areas.

1. Choose the Right Category

Select the category that best describes the issue, such as flooding, garbage accumulation, damaged sidewalks, broken streetlights, or electrical hazards.

2. Provide an Accurate Location

Enter the address or location of the problem. Include nearby landmarks when possible to make the location easier to identify.

3. Describe the Problem Clearly

Explain what happened, when you noticed the issue, and how it affects commuters. Provide accurate details and select the appropriate severity level.

4. Add Photo Evidence

If available, select a clear photo that shows the problem. Use JPG, PNG, or WebP images up to 10 MB. Never put yourself in danger to take a photo.

Important Reminders

  • Submit accurate and truthful information.
  • Avoid duplicate or misleading reports.
  • Do not include unnecessary sensitive personal information.
  • Keep your reference number to track your report.
  • For immediate dangers or emergencies, contact the appropriate emergency services instead.
Prototype Notice: CommuteCare is a student project. Reports are stored in your browser and are not sent to government agencies. The prototype only stores the selected photo's filename, not the actual image. Submitted reports are saved in Firebase Cloud Firestore, with a local browser copy for tracking.
