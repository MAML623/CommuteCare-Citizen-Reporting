Frequently Asked Questions

You can report garbage, flooding, damaged sidewalks, broken streetlights, and electrical hazards near stations, terminals, and other public transportation facilities.

Go to the Report page, choose the issue category, enter the location, describe the problem, attach evidence if you have any, then select Submit. You will receive a reference number after submitting.

Every report needs an issue category, the location (station, terminal, or street), a short description, and the date you noticed the problem. Photos or videos are optional but help reviewers act faster.

On the Report page, use the evidence upload field to attach clear photos of the issue. Make sure the problem and its surroundings are visible, and avoid including faces or personal information where possible.

Open the Track Report page and enter the reference number you received after submitting. The page shows the current status of your report, such as Received, Under Review, or Resolved.

A report may be rejected if it is a duplicate, outside the covered facilities, or lacks enough detail to verify. The tracking page shows the reason, and you may submit a new report with corrected information.

If reviewers request more details, open your report on the Track Report page using your reference number and follow the prompt to add notes or upload more evidence.
