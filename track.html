    Where is my reference number?

    You get it right after you submit a report on the Report an Issue page. It looks like CC-20261125-AB12C.

    Lost it? Reports you sent from this browser are listed in the dashboard.

    What the statuses mean

    • Pending saved from this browser, not checked yet.
    • Received CommuteCare sent it to the right office.
    • Under Review the office is checking the location and evidence.
    • Resolved the office fixed or closed the issue.
    • Rejected the report was closed. The reason is shown in the update.
