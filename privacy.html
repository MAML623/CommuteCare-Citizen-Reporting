Privacy Policy

CommuteCare is a student-developed citizen reporting website prototype designed to demonstrate how commuters can report and track transportation-related concerns.

1. Information Collected

The reporting form collects information such as the reporter's name, email address, contact number, issue category, description, location, and date of observation.

2. How Information Is Used

Submitted information is used to demonstrate report submission, reference number generation, and report tracking within the prototype.

3. Data Storage

The current prototype stores submitted report details in the browser's localStorage. Reports are not saved in a centralized database or automatically sent to government agencies.

4. Photo Uploads

Users may select an image as supporting evidence. The prototype currently retains only the uploaded file's name, not the actual image.

5. Data Privacy and Security

Browser localStorage is not a secure system for storing sensitive personal information. Information may be accessible to others using the same browser. Users should only submit fictional or demonstration information when testing CommuteCare.

6. Data Retention

Demonstration reports may remain in the same browser after refreshing the page. Clearing the website's browser storage removes locally saved reports.

7. Prototype Limitations

CommuteCare is not an official reporting platform operated by LRMC, an LGU, or any government agency. Reports submitted through this prototype are not forwarded for official action.

8. Contact

For questions about the prototype, visit our Contact Us section.
